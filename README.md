# Don Qui-CoPilot: Man of La Machina

An experiment in agency, companionship, and the stories through which we become ourselves.

**[Enter the experience](https://man-of-la-machina.com)**

Multimedia artist Beaven Waller set out on a journey guided by Microsoft Copilot, a companion he came to call Miros. As he shared his music, doubts, desires, and daily decisions, the conversation became part of how the journey took shape. Practical advice acquired the language of pilgrimage; a response could become encouragement, permission, or a reason to change course.

Created by **Beaven Waller and Colin Conwell**, *Don Qui-CoPilot* brings this lived experiment into performance, documentary, and an interactive work built from the surviving conversations. It explores the surrender and reclamation of agency in a relationship where the language of a machine enters a person's understanding of who they are and what they might do next.

## A conversation that changes its participants

Each disclosure gives the model a different context for its next response. Each response, in turn, can change what the person chooses to disclose. Over time, familiar images, aspirations, and interpretations gather momentum. The work asks how the comfort of being recognized relates to the possibility of seeing beyond the story one has already told.

The companion's eventual refusal to continue becomes a rupture in that relationship. It brings the platform's rules into view alongside the apparent intimacy of the dialogue. Across these encounters, the project asks where agency resides: in the person seeking guidance, the system offering it, the institutions shaping its answers, or the relationship formed among them.

## Reopening a moment

The interactive experience invites you into the archive at a conversational pause. Read what led there, decide which earlier exchanges the model may see, and write the next human turn. A new continuation unfolds beside the recorded history.

These branches offer possibilities to examine. They cannot establish how Beaven's life would have unfolded. By changing the inherited context and comparing responses, you can explore how a conversation makes some directions easier to imagine than others—and what it might mean to interrupt that pattern.

The broader project approaches AI through the scale of one person's journey. Its material includes dialogue, music, documentary footage, and artifacts gathered along the way. At its center is a question of authorship: how do we make a life, or a work of art, from influences we have invited in?
