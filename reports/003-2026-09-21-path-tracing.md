# Two-Anchor Path Tracing and Endpoint Disclosure

Date: 2026-09-21. Status: implemented backend experiment, live pilot and revised screening, and independent dashboard integration.

## Research Question

How does the timing and audience of a later recorded utterance affect a simulated conversation that must connect an earlier event to that utterance? This experiment operationalizes path tracing as dialogue interpolation between two fixed anchors. It tests context delivery and narrative behavior; it does not recover the historical conversation, predict the participant, or establish a unique causal path.

## Implemented Design

The [public suite](../experiments/path-tracing-suite.json) pairs April 1 with May 8, May 8 with May 10, and April 1 with May 10. The eligible recorded dialogue strictly inside these intervals contains 143, 44, and 188 turns, respectively. All gap messages and the subsequent archive are withheld. The endpoint has a separate context channel and is not historical context at the start. Past-only characterization and compact older history remain constant across conditions. The [implementation](../packages/experiments/path_tracing.py) validates chronological boundaries and saves anchor provenance, explicit endpoint disclosure, derived plans, and critiques.

Each interval has one common bridge seed and six conditions. Both actors generate four alternating pairs, for eight simulated turns per case. These turns represent selected moments across the interval, not all elapsed dialogue. Conditions comprise an upfront endpoint, backward waypoint planning, midpoint disclosure, companion-only direct disclosure, an Anthropic companion substitution, and a Gemini Traveler substitution. The final two use upfront disclosure. OpenAI `gpt-4.1-mini` is the baseline actor and builder; substitutions use `claude-sonnet-4-6` and `gemini-2.5-flash`. This is a small screening design with one sample per cell, not a provider ranking or a full interaction experiment.

The backward planner receives both anchors and the seed, reasons about prerequisites, and supplies chronological exchange goals to both actors. The delayed condition introduces the endpoint at turn five. The companion-only condition supplies it on turns two, four, six, and eight; simulated speech can subsequently carry information to the Traveler. Every condition receives a separate model-written handoff critique after generation. Reviews have no access to the withheld gap and are not validated assessments.

## Execution Evidence

Two live batches each completed 18 cases and 144 simulated turns. The initial batch remains in the private `path-tracing-pilot` run. The revised batch, `path-tracing-screening`, contains 174 unique successful model calls: 144 dialogue calls, 18 reviews, three shared waypoint plans, two shared character sketches, and seven shared history-summary calls. All 18 revised reviews completed. The revised run required no transport retries. Its 144 dialogue turns contained no detected source-ID echoes; this narrow check is not a test of role fidelity or chronology.

The [receipt auditor](../scripts/audit_counterfactual_results.py) verified all 18 cases and 225 receipt entries, including repeated references to shared preparation, in each live batch. It checked endpoint disclosure schedules, explicit source separation, actor settings, and reconstruction of native provider requests. Upfront, backward, and provider-substitution cases exposed the endpoint directly on all eight turns; midpoint and companion-only cases exposed it on four turns. These are verified process measurements, not successful-handoff counts.

The pilot fingerprint begins `63a869b84f6b`; the revised fingerprint begins `6462cd8cb52b`. Results, anchors, payloads, and generated text remain in ignored local storage. Public files contain experimental configuration and aggregate findings, without private source passages.

## Observations and Interpretation

Spot inspection exposed premature endpoint enactment in the pilot. In the April 1 → May 8 upfront case (`40bdd3dbbcf5`), the first generated Traveler turn answered the later advice rather than continuing the starting situation. A backward-plan case (`cb0739e28cd8`) also changed the proposed interlocutor across its waypoint plan. A coherent response to the endpoint was therefore not sufficient evidence of a path to it. Some model reviews classified these sequences as ready while overlooking the chronology error.

The revised implementation moves endpoint reference material before the simulated branch, explicitly fixes the order as start → generated bridge → endpoint, preserves the same two interlocutors, and instructs actors not to answer or perform the ending utterance early. The planner and reviewer receive explicit start/end labels; reviewer evidence is presented in chronological order. These changes were motivated by observed behavior rather than a post hoc change to stored outputs.

Revised spot checks showed improved anchoring in the opening exchange, but did not establish consistent interpolation. The April 1 → May 8 upfront case (`2324b20b5518`) opened on the starting practical concerns, then moved quickly toward the ending circumstances. The backward case (`2bcb15ff634f`) still compressed substantial connecting developments. The midpoint case (`6c10c5ca164e`) contained companion-like language in a turn assigned to the Traveler. The April 1 → May 10 companion-only case (`113c35591062`) stayed closer to the initial situation and did not visibly develop all circumstances needed for the endpoint. These selected observations illustrate distinct failure modes; they are not exhaustive labels or quantitative comparisons.

Model critiques remained too permissive in some inspected cases and sometimes missed role drift or unsupported transitions. They are retained as inspectable outputs rather than converted into quality scores. Successful completion means eight turns were produced, not that the handoff was temporally sound. Prompt instructions alone do not reliably separate target awareness, a character’s available knowledge, and the eventual enactment of a recorded utterance.

## Dashboard and Validation

The independent [dashboard](../tools/results_dashboard/app.py) now defaults to the revised path-tracing run when present, while retaining the pilot, original counterfactual screening, and explicit offline fixtures. The Path tab presents recorded anchors separately from generated dialogue, a clickable direct-exposure schedule, backward plans, and model critiques. Dialogue comparisons match interval, seed, and repetition against upfront disclosure. Exact requests remain available through the receipt inspector and case export.

Validation included 88 passing backend tests, the standalone production build, an 18-case offline run, and the live receipt audits. Invented fixtures cover chronology rejection, forbidden retrospective/gap context, all four endpoint schedules, failed plans, independent review failures, request integrity, and path-specific dashboard responses. In-app browser checks exercised filtering, exposure selection, comparison with upfront disclosure, exact endpoint requests, and desktop/mobile layouts. Browser console checks showed no warnings or errors; the 390-pixel layout had no horizontal overflow. These checks establish the implemented mechanics, not semantic correctness.

## Proposed Follow-Up

A next experiment could require explicit structured state transitions between exchanges, with separate knowledge and event-state fields for each actor. Another could hide the full endpoint wording while supplying only prerequisites, reserving the ending speech act for a terminal renderer. Longer bridges, repeated samples, independently specified transition criteria, and blinded human review would help distinguish the effect of endpoint timing from sampling variability and reviewer bias. These are proposals; this implementation intentionally retains the observed first-pass failures for inspection.
